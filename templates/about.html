What is here

The monthly LISTSERV logs of WPA-L from its years at Arizona State University, January 1993 to May 2021, as deposited on the Internet Archive ("WPA-L Archive 1993–2021 Complete Uncompressed"). The logs are split into messages, threaded, and indexed for search.

What is changed

  • Email addresses are removed from headers, bodies, signatures, and quoted lines.
  • Attachments are left out; a message lists the types it carried.
  • Names appear as posted. Where a From line had only an address, the name comes from the same address's other posts; if there were none, the message shows "Unnamed poster."
  • The site asks search engines not to index it.

How threads are rebuilt

From about 2010 the logs keep each message's Message-ID, so replies link to their parents directly. Before that, messages are grouped by subject line (ignoring "Re:" and "Fwd:") within a 30-day window, and a reply's parent is the earlier message whose author its "X wrote:" line names. Some long-running subjects will be split and some generic ones merged.
