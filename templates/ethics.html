Ethics

WPA-L was a semi-public professional space. Anyone could subscribe, but people were writing to colleagues, and most of those colleagues are still working in the field. So the reader follows a few rules, and the code enforces each of them.

What is shown

  • Messages as they were posted, with every email address taken out, and names as the posters wrote them.
  • A person page only for someone who posted at least {{ person_min }} messages.
  • A connection between two people (on person pages and in the network views) only when they exchanged at least {{ edge_min }} replies.
  • Descriptions of activity, never judgments of character. A chart of a busy thread shows how busy it was and says nothing about who was at fault.
  • A request that search engines leave every page out of their results, the API included. People find the archive by coming here to read it, and a colleague's old post doesn't turn up in a search for their name.

{{ ic('ic-optout', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} What withdrawal does

  • When someone withdraws, their messages come out of browsing, threads, search, permalinks, exports, saved corpora and the API. Their person page goes away, and their name drops out of every network, citation and institution listing.
  • Withdrawing one address or one spelling of a name withdraws the whole person, along with every address and spelling grouped with it.
  • Their messages still count, anonymously, in the totals: monthly volume, the baselines the term trends are measured against, and genre shares. Taking them out there would quietly change the shape of the record, and leaving them in reveals nothing about who wrote them.
  • Their words quoted inside other people's replies stay for now. How to handle those is still an open question.
  • None of this touches the Internet Archive, which keeps the original deposit.

If you posted to the list, you can ask to have your messages withdrawn at any time.

Exports

Before exporting anything, you'll be asked to agree to the terms of use. If your project is under IRB review, the pseudonymized export swaps names and person ids for stable pseudonyms. Names inside the message text itself (signatures, "X wrote:" lines) stay put, so handling those is up to you.

{{ ic('ic-redpen', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Corrections

If you spot two people grouped as one, a broken thread, or a bad match, you can report it from the page where you found it. Corrections are saved as decision files in the build, so they hold through every rebuild.
