{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Methodology

Only a poster's own words are read; quoted text is skipped so a reference repeated down a thread counts once. A DOI is matched exactly. A title in quotation marks or underscores (four words or more) is matched to a Pinakes title or main title, ignoring case and punctuation. “Author (year)” references are matched only when a single Pinakes work fits, and wait in a review queue. Pinakes indexes journal articles, so books, chapters and websites the list discussed are not matched here. Scholar names are Pinakes authors with three or more indexed works, named in full in a message they did not write.
