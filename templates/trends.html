{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Methodology

Messages containing counts messages whose subject or body matches the term in the search index, which stems words (“assess” also finds “assessment”). Occurrences counts the word or phrase itself, allowing up to three trailing letters for plurals and inflections. Rates divide by every message (or every word) posted that month or year, including the messages of posters who have withdrawn, since a total names no one.

Own words only searches each message with its quoted text and signature removed. Without it, a term in one message is counted again in every reply that quotes it, which inflates terms that start long threads. The quoted-text split is heuristic; see the Data page.
