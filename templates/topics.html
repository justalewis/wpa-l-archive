{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Model and reproducibility

Each thread is one document: its subject and its messages' own words, with quoted text and signatures removed. Documents are embedded with {{ run.config.embedding_model }}, reduced with UMAP and clustered with HDBSCAN (BERTopic), then merged to at most {{ run.config.reduce_to }} topics. {{ "{:,}".format(run.outliers) }} threads fit no topic and are left out. Topic words come from class-based TF-IDF.
