{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Methodology and evaluation

Replies are discussion, or debate when they fall in a thread of 30 or more messages from 15 or more people within two weeks. That is a structural definition, not a judgment of tone. Rules on the subject and opening words place calls for papers, job ads, announcements, tributes, list business, queries, and starters that comment on earlier posts. A logistic-regression model trained on the rule labels places the remaining thread starters when it is at least 55% sure; the rest are other.
