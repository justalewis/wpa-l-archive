{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} How this page is made

Messages are grouped into one person when they came from the same address (the address itself is not kept), or when the names on two addresses normalize to the same first and last name. Merges by name can join two different people who share a name; corrections are recorded in the build's decision files and survive rebuilds.

Affiliations are institution names that recur in this person's signature blocks, with the first and last year each appears. Pages exist only for people with at least 25 messages, and anyone can withdraw theirs through the opt-out form.
