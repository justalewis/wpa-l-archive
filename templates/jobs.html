{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Methodology

Job ads are messages labeled “job ad” on the Genres page that start a thread (replies about a job are left out). The kind comes from the ad's subject and opening: a writing center director or coordinator; a WPA (director of writing, composition or first-year writing); non-tenure-line (lecturer, instructor, visiting, adjunct, part-time); tenure-line (tenure-track, assistant, associate or full professor); postdocs and fellowships; and other. The first rule that matches wins, in that order. To find the school, every university, college or institute named in the subject or opening is collected, generic names ("College of Arts and Sciences," a bare "University of") are set aside, and the first remaining name that matches exactly one school in the IPEDS directory wins. An ad that names a partner school before its own can still be misattributed, and an ad posted twice counts twice.
