{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Methodology

Job ads are messages labeled “job ad” on the Genres page that start a thread (replies about a job are left out). The kind comes from the ad's subject and opening: a writing center director or coordinator; a WPA (director of writing, composition or first-year writing); non-tenure-line (lecturer, instructor, visiting, adjunct, part-time); tenure-line (tenure-track, assistant, associate or full professor); postdocs and fellowships; and other. The first rule that matches wins, in that order. The institution is the first university, college or institute named in the subject or opening, so an ad that names a partner school first can be misattributed. An ad posted twice counts twice.
