Messages per month

Each bar is one month of WPA-L, from the first saved log in January 1993 to the last in May 2021. The list grew steadily through the 1990s, stayed busy through most of the 2000s, and peaked in 2013 at more than 10,500 messages before tapering off in its last years. Hover over a bar to see the month's count, or click it to read that month's threads.

The numbered markers under the chart are moments from the field's calendar, and the list just below the chart says what each one was and how busy the list was that month.
