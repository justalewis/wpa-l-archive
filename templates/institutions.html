{{ ic('ic-method', 'ic-sm ic-quiet') }} Methodology

Names are matched to the 2022 IPEDS directory (NCES): first by a reviewed decision, then exactly on a normalized name or IPEDS alias, then by a fuzzy match accepted only when a single institution contains every word of the name. Carnegie types are the 2021 basic classification in eight groups. A message counts toward an institution when its author's signatures name that institution in the period the message falls in. Signatures under-represent part-time and contingent faculty, who are less likely to sign with an institution, so these shares describe the visible, signed voices on the list.
